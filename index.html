Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

What we store on your device

Skrambeasts saves your game progress locally in your browser's storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device unless you use the in-game Export Save Code feature yourself.

What we store to enable multiplayer features

To support Friends, the Leaderboard, and Player VS Player features, the game creates a randomly-generated player ID and an anonymous authentication identity (via Firebase, a Google service) for your device. This is not linked to your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. Your chosen in-game username, your Hunter Rank and stats, and social requests (friends, duels) are stored under this anonymous ID.
